Delete your account

DRAFT, pending review by the project team. This page has not yet been reviewed. The steps below match the app as built.

You can delete your GuardBand account yourself, from inside the app. It is permanent and there is no undo, no grace period and no recovery window — once it is done, it is done.

In the app

Track tab → the gear icon, top right → Settings → Delete account
  1. Open GuardBand and sign in, if you are not already.
  2. On the Track tab, tap the gear icon in the top bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, in red.
  4. Read what the confirmation says will be deleted, then tap Delete account again.
  5. Confirm it is you. If you sign in with a password, you will be asked to type it. If you sign in with Google, you will be asked to pick your Google account again — pick the same one you signed in with.

The app then deletes your data, deletes your sign-in, and returns you to the sign-in screen. Leave it on screen while it works.

If it stops part way — the connection drops, say — the screen shows what is left to do and a Retry button. Tap it. Retry picks up where it stopped rather than starting over, and it is worth finishing: if you walk away mid-delete, your sign-in may still exist even though your data is gone.

What is deleted

  • Your profile — your name and email address.
  • Every emergency contact you saved, including their names, phone numbers and relationships.
  • Your consent record.
  • Your sign-in, so that email address can be used to make a new account later.

What is not deleted

The alerts and tracking updates the band recorded stay.

GuardBand does not have device pairing yet, so every installation reports under one shared band identifier rather than one tied to your account. Those records are not linked to you, and there is no way for the app to pick out which of them were yours. So there is nothing there it can delete on your behalf — and nothing there that identifies you either.

What is in those records is a position, a battery level and a timestamp. No name, no email, no phone number.

Google may also keep its own operational logs of the sign-in service for its own retention periods, which the project does not control.

If you cannot open the app

If you have lost your password, try Forgot Password? on the sign-in screen first — that gets you back in, and then the steps above work.

If you still cannot get in, or you have already uninstalled the app, write to julianmiguelroldan@gmail.com from the email address on the account and ask for it to be deleted.

We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. We only act on a request sent from the email address on the account, because that is how we know it is yours. A request from any other address gets a reply asking you to write from the right one. The same things are deleted, and kept, as listed above.
